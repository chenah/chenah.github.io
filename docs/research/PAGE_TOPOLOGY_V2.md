# Page topology V2

1. `Nav` — fixed identity, active section state, expandable full-screen navigation.
2. `Hero` — full-viewport identity stage with a five-band interactive portrait.
3. `About` — dark manifesto, biography, skills, and metrics.
4. `ResearchStory` — sticky Observe / Prototype / Validate research narrative.
5. `CtaBand` — oversized paths into publications and projects.
6. `Publications` — editorial paper index.
7. `Experience` — dark asymmetric timeline.
8. `Projects` — applied-research case studies.
9. `Marquee` — kinetic affiliation strip.
10. `Footer` — contact statement and external profiles.

The document uses Lenis scrolling. The new story section supplies the long-form editorial rhythm seen in the reference site's message/collage sequence while retaining original academic content and assets.
